Empower The Girl Child, Advance The Nation

Every Nigerian girl deserves a fair shot at the life she wants.

Girl Child Care Advancement Initiative (GCCAI) helps girls get past the financial, social and cultural barriers that keep them from school, safety, leadership and opportunity.

Why we exist

Too many girls lose options before they get to choose.

1 in 3

Nigerian women aged 20 to 24 were married before they turned 18.

Source: Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey 2023–24 (33.4%).

7.6m

girls in Nigeria are out of school, about half of them in the North.

Source: UNICEF estimate.

Under 5%

of seats in the National Assembly are held by women: about 20 out of 469.

Source: 10th National Assembly, elected 2023.

What we believe

Girls are not a project. They are people with plans.

We start with the girl: what she wants, what is in her way, and what she already has. Then we work on the barriers, from school costs to the silence around violence, so she has more say over her own future.

Girls are not only future mothers, workers or leaders. They have rights and ideas now.
